# Stilo Support Help with writing, notebooks, exports, and backups in Stilo for iPhone and iPad. ## Contact support For technical help, bug reports, feature requests, or privacy questions, email [tangrenlive@outlook.com](mailto:tangrenlive@outlook.com). Suggested subject: **Stilo Support — Brief description of your issue** To help us understand a problem, include: - Your device model and iOS or iPadOS version. - The Stilo version and build number, shown at the bottom of Settings. - What you were trying to do, what happened, and what you expected. - Steps to reproduce the issue, if you know them. - A screenshot with personal information removed, if it would help. Please do not send passwords, private writing, or a full library backup. A short example using fictional text is usually enough to explain a writing or formatting problem. ## Getting started Tap Quick Note to begin, or choose New Notebook to create a notebook with your preferred title, cover, and paper. Open a notebook and start writing. If you are in reading mode, tap Edit to return to writing. Use the Typewriters tab in the library to choose Classic Black or Ivory Electronic. In Settings, you can turn Typing Sounds and Haptics on or off. Stilo requires iOS or iPadOS 17 or later. The app interface is in English. ## Writing in other languages Open Settings from the library and enable System Keyboard to use your device's keyboard and installed input methods. This allows you to type in Chinese and other languages supported by your keyboard. The available characters and their appearance also depend on the selected font and system font fallback. ## Pages, formatting, and search In an open notebook, tap the sheet counter to open Pages. Choose Add Page to insert a sheet after the current page. New pages inherit the current paper, font, and ribbon settings. Use the editor controls to choose a typeface, paper, text formatting, and black or red ribbon ink. Formatting can apply to selected text and future typing. Undo and redo are available during the current editing session. Use Find to search the current page, or the library's Search tab to find matching notebook titles and page text. ## Exporting a notebook Open a notebook's options menu in the library and choose Export PDF or Export Text. Choose a destination from the system share sheet. - **PDF** is for reading, printing, and sharing formatted pages. - **TXT** is a plain-text copy of your writing. - **JSON library backup** is the format to use when you need to restore editable notebooks and library settings in Stilo. PDF and TXT exports cannot be used as library restore files. ## Backing up and transferring your library Your notebooks are stored locally. Stilo does not automatically sync them between devices. To create a backup: 1. Open Settings from the library. 2. Choose Export Library Backup. 3. Save the JSON file to Files or another location you control. Keep regular backups, especially before deleting the app, replacing a device, or restoring another library. A library backup may contain deleted items retained in Recently Deleted; store it somewhere private. To restore or transfer a library: 1. Make the JSON backup available on the destination device. 2. Open Stilo, go to Settings, and choose Restore Backup. 3. Select a compatible Stilo JSON backup and review the notebook and page counts. 4. Confirm the restore. **Restoring replaces the entire current library; it does not merge libraries.** Stilo keeps a recovery copy of the previous library, available under Previous Libraries in Settings. Export a separate backup of your current library before restoring. Backup imports support compatible JSON files up to 50 MB. This is an import limit, not a promise of unlimited library size or performance. ## Recovering deleted writing Open Recently Deleted from the library or Settings to restore a deleted notebook or page. Permanently deleting an item removes it from the current library, but older recovery copies and exported backups may still contain it. Previous Libraries in Settings can provide an earlier saved library or a copy kept before a restore. Restoring one replaces the current library after confirmation. Use Delete Recovery Copies in Settings to remove those local recovery files. Exported copies and device backups must be managed separately. ## If changes are not saving Check the save status in Settings. If Stilo reports unsaved changes: 1. Check that your device has available storage. 2. Use Retry Save, or the retry action shown with the save warning. 3. If possible, export your current writing before closing the app. 4. Contact support if the problem continues. If the library cannot be read, editing may be paused to preserve the original file. Use Export Original Library in Settings before attempting recovery. Check Previous Libraries or restore a known compatible backup. **Do not delete and reinstall Stilo as a troubleshooting step unless you have a usable backup.** Deleting the app removes its local library and recovery files. The developer cannot remotely recover writing stored only on your device. ## If backup import or export fails For imports, choose an original Stilo JSON library backup, confirm it is within the 50 MB limit, and make sure the file is fully downloaded from its storage provider. Renaming a PDF or TXT file to JSON does not make it a compatible backup. For exports, check available storage and try another destination in the system share sheet. If a problem continues, email the error message and steps you followed, without attaching your private library. ## Sounds and haptics Typing sounds respect silent mode. If sounds are missing, check Typing Sounds in Stilo Settings, your device's silent setting, volume, and audio output destination. Haptic feedback requires a supported device and appropriate system settings. It may not be available on your device, including iPad models without the required hardware. ## Privacy Stilo does not require an account and has no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs. Writing and preferences stay in the app's local storage unless you choose to export or share files, or your device includes them in a backup. For details about local storage, backups, deletion, and support correspondence, see the **Stilo Privacy Policy** published with this support page. For privacy questions, email [tangrenlive@outlook.com](mailto:tangrenlive@outlook.com).
